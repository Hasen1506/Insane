INSANE v0.7

The barter-and-dare app for the five people you actually see. This is the working build, not the mock-up: everything you do here is saved and shared with everyone you invite.

What's real here

RealSign-in. You're you through your Claude account. RealCircles, threads, plans, pots, journeys, feed, calls, Decode, drops. Live between everyone invited. RealDraws are worked out in the open from who turned up, so anyone can check them. Stand-inMap: drawn 3 km zones. Map tiles can't load in this frame. The phone app uses MapLibre with OpenStreetMap. Stand-inLocation: you pick your area. The phone app checks GPS only at a drop. Stand-inMeeting in person: a 6-digit code valid for 2 minutes. The phone app adds a QR scan and a Bluetooth check. Stand-inGo dark covers this app only. The phone app uses Screen Time to cover the apps you pick. Stand-inNo push notifications. Badges show what's new.

Playing with friends

Invite each person by email from the Share menu. Anyone who can only view can look around but can't post.
