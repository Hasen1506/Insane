INSANE v0.8

The barter-and-dare app for the five people you actually see. This is the working build, not the mock-up: everything you do here is saved and shared with everyone you invite.

What's real here

RealPlaces anywhere on Earth: 69,818 towns, a world map with roads, a pin you can drop to the metre. RealPrivacy you choose: an exact pin only you can read, or a 1, 4 or 9 km² square. Drops only show within the distance you set. RealSign-in, circles, threads, plans, journeys, feed, calls, Decode, drops, QR meeting codes. Live between everyone invited. Realv0.6’s game, back: favors in hours with a −5 h limit, pots with who’s paid, the daily dare and the jar, photo clips gone in 24 h, cheers, the Mole, Missions with a hidden spot, trails stamped by GPS. RealDraws worked out in the open from who turned up. Door codes kept only as a slow hash. Limitclaude.ai pages can't load street tiles, read GPS or send notifications. The INSANE server version (same app) does all three, and checks codes and answers itself.
