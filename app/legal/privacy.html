Privacy notice

Draft, not legal advice. Written by the INSANE team for the v0.9 test and not yet reviewed by a lawyer. Consent version 2026-10-draft. Parts marked needs Vijay are placeholders.

This notice is given under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules. It tells you what we collect, why, and your rights.

Data fiduciary

needs Vijay: legal name and contact address.

What we collect, and why

  • Your email: to sign you in and to send you sign-in codes and important notices.
  • Your name and handle: so your circle knows who you are (name) and the city sees a handle instead.
  • Your area: a 1–9 km² square and your city, to show drops and people near you. If you choose an exact pin it stays private to you. We keep no location history.
  • What you write: messages, plans, pots, favors, dares, Midnight and sealed messages, posts, and your private journeys and timeline. Midnight messages are stored without your name, by design.
  • Your age confirmation and consent record: that you said you’re old enough, which version of these terms you agreed to, and when.
  • Devices you’re signed in on: browser and system type and when they were last used, so you can sign them out.
  • Steps you’ve reached: for example “joined a circle” and which days you opened the app, to learn what works. No advertising, no tracking across other sites, and we don’t sell data.
  • Reports and blocks: who you blocked (so it can be enforced) and reports you send (so we can act on them).

Who sees it

Your circle sees what you share in it. Your city sees your handle and city posts. We use processors to run the service: hosting (needs Vijay: e.g. Render), email delivery (needs Vijay: e.g. Resend) and error monitoring (needs Vijay: e.g. Sentry, with personal data scrubbed). Data may be processed outside India where the law allows it.

How long we keep it

While your account exists. When you delete it, we erase your profile, private space, posts, progress, memberships, sessions and notification subscriptions in one step, and email you to confirm. Messages you sent in group threads stay, signed “Former member”, so other people’s conversations still make sense. Backups roll over within needs Vijay: e.g. 30 days.

Your rights

You can access and download your data (You → Download my data), correct it (Edit profile, Change email), withdraw consent and erase it (Delete everything), nominate someone to act for you, and complain to our grievance officer below and then to the Data Protection Board of India. Withdrawing consent means deleting your account, since INSANE can’t work without the data above.

Children

INSANE isn’t for children: 18+ in India, 16+ elsewhere. We don’t knowingly process children’s data.

Security

Sessions are stored hashed. Storage rules on the server decide who can read each record. If a breach affects you, we’ll tell you and the Board as the law requires.
