Community rules

Draft, not legal advice. Written by the INSANE team for the v0.9 test and not yet reviewed by a lawyer. Consent version 2026-10-draft. Parts marked needs Vijay are placeholders.

INSANE is for the people you actually see. These rules keep it that way.

People

  • No harassment, threats, bullying, stalking or hate.
  • Don’t share anyone’s private information, location or photos without their say-so.
  • Don’t pretend to be someone else.
  • No sexual content involving anyone who didn’t agree, and nothing sexual involving minors, ever.

Midnight

  • Midnight is about you. Names of people in your circle are blocked, first names and handles included.
  • Anyone in the circle can remove a Midnight message for everyone, no reason needed.

Dares

  • Saying no is always fine. No reason needed, no penalty.
  • Nothing that could hurt anyone: no traffic or trains, fire, heights, alcohol or drugs, police or the law, money, weapons, or strangers’ bodies. INSANE refuses these outright.
  • The jar goes up to “bold 6”. Anything wilder needs a venue with staff.
  • Tap ⚑ Not safe on any dare. One flag hides it for you; two take it out of the jar for the circle, and the owner reviews it.

Meeting up

  • Meet in public places. Tell someone where you’re going.
  • Leave whenever you want.

What happens if you break them

Anyone can block or report you. Circle owners can remove members. We may remove content, and suspend or close accounts. Serious threats go to the police.
