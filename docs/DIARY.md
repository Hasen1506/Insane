# 30 days as a user

A diary study done as a cognitive walkthrough. I played one realistic person, opening the app (or not) every day for 30 days, first on v0.1 and then on the redesigned v0.2. Every v0.1 event below is something the v0.1 prototype actually does. Minutes are time spent in the app.

**The user:** 26, shares a flat in a mid-size city, has more stuff than cash, works 9–6. Three friends were willing to install the app. Not especially bold.

**Totals:** opened on 16 of 30 days, about 70 minutes in all. One dare done. Zero swaps completed (one half-failed). One IOU now owned by a stranger.

### What worked
- **Dares from friends, with stakes I actually want.** Day 4 was the best moment of the month.
- **Watching people I know.** It was the last reason I kept opening.
- **Favors for services.** The guitar lesson was the best exchange of the month, and it wasn't a physical item.

### What broke, and where
| Problem | Diary moment | Root cause |
|---|---|---|
| Nothing to match | Day 1 | Cold start: two friends is below the atomic network |
| Nothing to do most days | Days 3, 11, 13, 22+ | Frequency mismatch: I have something new to swap about once a month |
| Loop half-failed | Days 7–9 | Three separate handoffs, no simultaneous exchange |
| Trust decay felt like a trick | Days 2, 12 | Punishes absence; locks the core feature behind it |
| Blamed for a broken chain | Day 10 | A two-hour window punishes anyone with a job |
| IOU sold to a stranger | Day 14 | Debt left the friend group: a betrayal, not a feature |
| Four sliders | Day 1 | Too much effort per rating, so people stop rating honestly |

The surprise: **the dystopia never arrived, because the app never got dense enough to have one.** v0.1 doesn't die from addiction. It dies from loneliness, logistics, and a few mechanics that feel like punishments.

**Totals:** opened on 26 of 30 days, about 120 minutes in all, plus roughly two hours of offline meetups. Six swaps completed, four favors done, one chain skipped without blame. Still opening on day 30.

### New risks v0.2 introduces
- **Novelty decay of the Daily Dare** (BeReal's curve). Mitigated by circle-written prompts and circle-vs-circle nights, not solved.
- **Peer pressure inside a small circle.** Fewer strangers, more people whose opinion I care about.
- **Market Day needs everyone in one city.** Long-distance friends are back to favors only.
- **The circle is the product.** If a circle dies, everyone in it churns together.

## Real-world test plan

Before writing more code, run v0.2 as a concierge test: a group chat per circle, with a human acting as the matcher.

- **Who:** three friend circles of 8–12 people each, ideally one student group, one flatshare, one group of parents.
- **How long:** two weeks, which includes two Market Days.
- **Setup:** a shared spreadsheet for listings and the ledger. One person runs the matcher by hand. Daily Dare is posted in the chat at the same time each day.

Pass/fail thresholds, set in advance:

| Metric | Pass |
|---|---|
| Members who complete at least one exchange (swap or favor) | ≥ 60% |
| Members who post on day 14 | ≥ 25% |
| Loops cleared per circle per Market Day | ≥ 3 |
| Daily Dare participation in week 2, as a share of week 1 | ≥ 70% |
| Members who say a mechanic felt like a punishment | ≤ 1 per circle |

If two or more thresholds fail, the design changes before any code is written.
