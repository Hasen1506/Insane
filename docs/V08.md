# v0.8: the real app, with v0.6's game back in it

This round answers two requests:

1. **"Why are there still stand-ins?"** Make places work anywhere on Earth, let people choose an exact private pin or a zone of a few km², stop drops from different regions mixing, make every feature real, and test it like a real user with a real login over many days.
2. **"A lot of v0.6 seems to be missing."** Bring back v0.6's flow and features: they were easier to navigate and explore, and richer.

## Why v0.6's features went missing

v0.7 was a rebuild, not an upgrade. The prompt for it ([`BUILD.md`](BUILD.md) §1) said to build *one loop end to end* and to label every stand-in. I kept that literally. Anything that needed new data, new storage rules or a check the page can't be trusted with was cut, not faked. That cut:

- the Ledger (favors in hours, pots with who's paid);
- the Mole;
- the daily dare, the dare jar and the 24-hour clips;
- Missions and trails;
- cheers, Midnight reactions, feed reactions;
- the five-tab layout with the **+** button.

Those were what made v0.6 feel alive. The cut was the wrong trade-off. They're back now, and each one is real: saved, shared live with the circle, and checked by the server where the page can't be trusted.

## What v0.8 is now

**Navigation (v0.6's):** Home · Circle · **+** · City · Ledger. Your profile, journeys and settings sit behind your initial in the top corner.

| | What it does | Where it's enforced |
|---|---|---|
| **Home** | Today's date, "N things today", then one list: pacts due, the Decode, the daily dare, new messages, **Needs you** (favors to do or confirm, pots to pledge or pay, plans to answer, dares sent to you, Mole voting, Missions to confirm), then **Your circle** (Midnight, the Mole, who's going dark) | |
| **+** | 15 things to make, each opening the right form | |

| **Circle → Pacts** | Your pacts with the stake you chose; everyone's counts with **Cheer** | Cheers can only be sent as yourself |

INSANE never holds money |

**Onboarding (v0.6's, plus a real place):**
1. Your name and a city-facing handle.
2. Your place: GPS, search, a pasted maps link or a tap on the map; an exact private pin or a 1, 4 or 9 km² square; how far you'd go for a drop.
3. Start or join a circle, with "scan each other's phones" explained.
4. **What's on your mind:** Need / Want / Urge tabs, one line in your own words, how INSANE reads it, and a stake if it's a pact.

If you arrive by an invite QR, the circle's name greets you ("Night Shift brought you in").

**Go dark:** 1 to 24 hours, now or scheduled (tonight, tomorrow, Sunday 10:00). Optionally with the circle: they get a message and a **Join them** card. The dark screen shows who's dark with you. When you come back early, the circle sees "came back after 1h of 6".

## Location: anywhere, as exact as you choose

- **Places:**
  - 69,818 towns of 5,000+ people, bundled with the app.
  - Street-level search through OpenStreetMap on the server version.
  - GPS, pasted Google, Apple or OpenStreetMap links, plain coordinates.
  - A tap on the map.
- **Privacy:**
  - **Exact** keeps your pin in your private space, which nobody else can read (checked by asking the server directly with another person's session). Others see a 1 km² square.
  - **Zone** stores no pin at all, just a 1, 4 or 9 km² square.
- **No mixing:**
  - Drops are filed under 25 km squares and filtered to the distance you set (2, 5, 10 or 25 km).
  - Your city for the feed, Decode and League is the nearest city of a million or more within 35 km. Otherwise it's the nearest of 300,000+, then the nearest of 50,000+.
  - So Tokyo's wards share Tokyo, Ikeja shares Lagos and Amadora shares Lisbon.

## The server version does what claude.ai can't

[`server/server.js`](../server/server.js) serves the same `app/index.html`. It's Node 22 with no dependencies and stores data in SQLite.

- Email sign-in with a 6-digit code.
- Live sync over server-sent events.
- OpenStreetMap street tiles and search.
- GPS.
- Web push, held in quiet hours and while you're dark.
- The checks a page can't be trusted with:
  - door codes;
  - Decode answers;
  - meeting codes;
  - Midnight and sealed text until release;
  - Mission spots;
  - favor and pot rules;
  - the Mole's identity;
  - trail windows;
  - clip contents.

```
DEV=1 OWNER_EMAIL=you@example.com node server/server.js        # codes print to the log
RESEND_API_KEY=… MAIL_FROM='INSANE <hi@yourdomain>' PUBLIC_URL=https://yourdomain node server/server.js   # real email
TILE_URL='https://{s}.your-tiles/{z}/{x}/{y}.png' TILE_ATTR='© …'   # your own tile provider for real traffic
```

Inside claude.ai the same app runs on the artifact's shared store. The limits there are the frame's, and each is labelled in the app:

- no street tiles (it draws the bundled world map instead);
- no GPS (trail stamps are "on your word" and marked so);
- no push notifications;
- Decode answers, unopened Midnight messages and Mission spots are hidden only by the page.

## Simulator vs playtest

- **The simulator** ([`prototype/index.html`](../prototype/index.html)) models thousands of synthetic people deciding whether to open the app. It answers "which design keeps more people", and it never touches the real app.
- **The playtest** ([`server/test/playtest.js`](../server/test/playtest.js)) drives the real app in real browsers against the real server, one person per browser, with emulated GPS and time zones. The server's clock and every browser's clock move together through 14 days. It answers "does it actually work, for a person, over time".

## Results

- **Playtest, server version: 92/92.** Eleven people in Lisbon, Tokyo, Lagos and São Paulo over 14 simulated days. They sign in by emailed code, onboard, and use plans, favors, pots, daily dares with photos, cheers, the Decode, drops and draws, Midnight, a Mission with a hidden spot, a trail stamped by GPS, meeting by QR, the Mole, calls, going dark and calm mode, then export, delete and sign out. Along the way it runs attacker checks straight against the API with a real session. The diary and every check are in [`PLAYTEST.md`](PLAYTEST.md), with screenshots in [`playtest/`](playtest/).
- **Local test (claude.ai and demo store): 83/83.** Seven people in one browser cover the same features on the store the artifact uses, plus a run under the artifact's content security policy with no errors.

## What the playtest found, and what changed because of it

| Finding | Fix |
|---|---|
| Kōenji resolved to "Nakano", and Ikeja to "Ikeja", so Tokyo's wards and Lagos's suburbs each got their own Decode and feed | A city of a million or more within 35 km wins |
| Tapping Stamp while the browser's location prompt was still open hung forever (a pending prompt never times out) | INSANE gives up after 8 s and stamps on your word, marked as such |
| A Mission's exact spot showed on the card days early for people in other circles (local store) | The page shows the spot only to the host circle, or to people who said yes from 2 h before. The server also strips it from the data |
| The trail map cropped the first stop | Zoom fits all stops |
| Old-style pots showed £ in Lisbon | Pots use the local currency |
| Calls in Lagos can't be answered: there's only one circle there | Not a bug. Calls need a second circle nearby, so seeding a city with two circles matters |
| Feed posts and calls are locked until two people in the circle have scanned each other's phones | Kept on purpose (v0.6's anti-bot rule). The lock says how to unlock it, with a Meet button |

## What's still not built from v0.6, and why

- **Zones and fixtures** (circle vs circle at a venue with staff) need real venue partners, so there's nothing honest to build yet. The League stays points-only.
- **Chapters** (invite-only clubs across circles) and the **one-invite-a-month vouch with Reliability** weren't ported. Reliability shows as a host's record ("hosted 4, flagged 0") with a stake on Missions.
- **Clips are photos, not 15-second videos.** Video is too heavy for either store. Photos are shrunk on the phone to about 50 KB.
- **Go dark can't lock other apps.** No web app can, and on a phone only Screen Time or Digital Wellbeing can. INSANE goes blank, holds notifications, and tells you how to set a Focus for the same hours.
- **Unlocking features over the first month** (v0.6's gradual reveal) isn't in. Everything is visible from day one, so a real two-week test should watch whether that overwhelms people.
