# v0.4: Pots, Chapters, Missions

v0.4 builds the three ideas that ranked highest when every idea from [`OPTIONS.md`](OPTIONS.md) was simulated ([`IMPACT.md`](IMPACT.md)): pledge-then-pay **Pots**, **Chapters** and **Missions**. Everything from v0.3 stays. The phone in [`prototype/index.html`](../prototype/index.html) has all three working, and v0.3 is kept at [`prototype/v3/`](../prototype/v3/index.html).

Model estimate: day 30 rises from about 59% (v0.3) to about 69%. Trust the ranking more than the level; see the caveats in IMPACT.md.

## Pots (Money → Pots)

Two kinds, both ledger-only:

| | Split pot | Pledge, then pay |
|---|---|---|
| For | A trip someone is already booking | Buying something together |
| How | Everyone pays the organizer as they go; the organizer confirms it landed | Nobody pays unless enough people pledge by the deadline. When the goal is met, everyone pays the shop or organizer directly |
| If it falls through | The organizer sorts out refunds person to person | The pot just closes. Nothing moved, so there's nothing to refund |

**Rules**
- **INSANE never holds money.** No balance, no top-up, no withdrawal, no card. A payment button opens the person's own bank app, PayPal or Revolut, or records cash in hand. The app only shows the tick.
- **The organizer confirms** each payment. The app never claims money arrived.
- **One message per pot** to the circle. Reminders come from people ("Ask Jules"), not from the app.
- **Favors and money never mix.** Pots are in real currency and favors stay favors. Neither converts into the other.

## Chapters (Chapter tab)

Invite-only clubs that cross circles. Your circle is who you share a fridge with; your chapter is who you go big with.

- **The rules:** videos live in the Vault and vanish after 24 hours; you vouch for who you bring in; nobody is ever the target; at chapter night you go by your handle.
- **Chapter night** happens in a Zone's back room after the fixture (roast battles, arm-wrestling, Homework stories), so it inherits the Zone's staff and insanity ceiling.
- **The Vault** is for members only: no downloads, no sharing. The person in a video is told if someone screenshots it.
- **Vouching:** one invite a month. You stake 1 Reliability on the person you bring in. If they flake within 21 days you lose it; if they stick, you both get it back plus one. Newcomers see nothing from before they joined.
- **Why it needs Zones:** in the model, Chapters add nothing to retention without chapter nights. A private club with nowhere to meet is just a smaller feed.

## Missions (City map and Chapter tab)

Monthly city-wide stunts with no victims. Mission 07 is "The Freeze": 60 seconds of everyone in a square stopping mid-motion.

- **Secrecy:** the map shows an approximate area. The exact spot and your personal briefing arrive two hours before.
- **Rules:** no property damage, nobody is the target, a permit is filed with the city for any crowd, and you leave the place as you found it.
- **Never owed:** dropping out costs nothing. Missions are the growth idea (the most new sign-ups in the model), not a retention hook, so they never create obligation.
