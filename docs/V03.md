# v0.3: the road to 90%

The question: *what would it take to go from 23% to 90%? Money drops? Treasure hunts? Record zones with cameras?*

## The honest ceiling

"90%" can mean two very different things.

| Measure | What it means | Published reference | v0.2 | v0.3 (model) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Day 30** | Opened on exactly day 30 after joining | Social apps: 4–10% | 23% | **≈ 58%** |
| **Month one** | Opened on any day from 23 to 30 | WhatsApp ≈ 86% on a stricter measure (Sensor Tower, secondhand, unverified) | 76% | **≈ 96%** |

- **No app keeps 90% of people opening it on a given day 30.** v0.3's ~58% is already far above every published range, so treat it as the optimistic end of a toy model.
- **90% "still around in month one" is real, but only for infrastructure.** WhatsApp gets there because leaving means losing the people you talk to, not because of streaks or prizes. The model's month-one measure is looser than Sensor Tower's: even a plain app with no hooks scores about 39% on it. Compare against that, not against WhatsApp.

So the answer to "how do we get to 90%" is: **stop being only an entertainment app and become the thing your circle runs on.** Then add the spectacle that earns the name.

## Your three ideas

| Idea | Verdict | Evidence | Simulator |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Random money drops** | **No.** Replaced by drops of *real stuff* that need three of your circle in one place to claim | HQ Trivia was exactly this: 2.3M+ concurrent players in Feb 2018, then prizes split into cents, cheaters, repetition. Shut down in 2020 after paying out ~$6M of $15M+ funding; the last prize was $5 shared by 523 people. In a post-money world, cash drops are also free issuance: the trueque/Bunz mistake | Drops paid in favors: favor confidence 85% → 51%, swaps/week 242 → 180, day 30 58% → 55% |
| **Treasure hunt constructions** | **Yes, changed:** circles build hunts *for other circles* | Pokémon GO peaked at ~45M daily users in July 2016 and was ~30M by late summer. Company-made content runs out of novelty; player-made content doesn't | Removing hunts: day 30 58% → 55%. Recruitment and stories, not retention |
| **Record zones with cameras** | **Yes, the best idea in the project.** Zones are the stage the name was waiting for | Solves safety (the big stuff happens where someone is watching), quality (fixed cameras, verified entries) and gathering (a weekly night out) at once | Removing Zones: day 30 58% → 52%. Removing the ceiling everywhere: people hurt 6 → ~690 in 120 days |

## What v0.3 adds

### Infrastructure: why people can't leave
1. **The ledger is the circle's money.** Groceries split six ways, dinners cooked, parcels signed for, tools borrowed: all settled in favors. You open it for the same reason you open a banking app. *Biggest single step in the model: day 30 goes from 22% to 41%.*
2. **The League.** Circle vs circle, a fixture every week, promotion and relegation over an eight-week season. If your lineup is short, your circle forfeits. Teammates notice when you're missing and pull you back, which no notification can do. *Model: −4 points if removed from v0.3.*

### Spectacle: why the app is called INSANE
3. **Zones.** Partner venues (skateparks, gyms, a bar's back room, community halls) with fixed cameras and staff. Weekly **Zone Night**. The insanity ceiling rises from 6.5 to **8 only inside Zones**. Zones also host the Market and League fixtures.
4. **Hunts.** Built by circles for other circles. Each clue is a 10-second dare filmed at a real spot; the final box holds something from the builder's Market pool.
5. **Drops.** Real items from local shops that want foot traffic (never favors, never cash). Claiming needs three of your circle in the same place within a time window.
6. **Insane Week.** Once a season: every circle in the city, one weekend, Zone finals.

### Rules that keep it alive
- **The map never shows where people are.** Only places and things.
- Above 6.5 only happens in Zones, with staff. A danger flag still removes reward everywhere.
- Drops are real goods, never favors or cash.
- Everything from v0.2 stays: circles, credit limit, jubilee, no decay, 24-hour chains, free decline.

## Where the model disagrees with the diary (again)
Adding trust decay to v0.3 raises simulated day 30 to ~65%. The model counts opens, not how they feel. In the diary it felt like a trick, so it stays off and goes into the real-world test as an explicit A/B.

## New risks
- **Physical gatherings:** liability, venue partnerships, permits, and crowd safety at Zone Nights and Insane Week.
- **Location features:** hunts and drops must never reveal people's locations, and drops need claim windows that don't encourage rushing through traffic.
- **The ledger as money:** disputes, fairness and possibly regulation once favors settle real household costs.
- **Team pressure:** the League's best retention lever is also peer pressure. Forfeits must never be blamed on one person by name.
- **Minors:** Zones, Hunts and Drops are 18+. Under-18s keep the ledger and favors only.

## Real-world test, extended
The two-week concierge test from [`DIARY.md`](DIARY.md#real-world-test-plan), plus:
- One partner Zone (a skatepark or community hall) for two Zone Nights.
- One circle-built hunt per circle.
- The ledger used for real shared expenses for both weeks.
- One circle with trust decay, two without.

**Pass if:** at least 80% of circle members open the app in week 2; every circle fields a full League lineup both weeks; zero injuries; and no more than one person per circle says a mechanic felt like a punishment.
