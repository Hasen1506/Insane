# WS-E: game integrity and content (v0.9)

Closes C48, C50, C86, C91, C92 and C93 from [CRITIQUES-V09](CRITIQUES-V09.md). Plan: [PLAN-V09](PLAN-V09.md) §3 WS-E.

## Decode: the page never has the answer (C48, C86)

- **Server mode.** The server holds every puzzle (`server/content.js`, seed packs in `server/content/puzzles.json`, the owner's additions in the `content_items` table, which is not part of the document store).
  - `GET /api/decode?week=` returns the clue, only the hints that are out, the solve count and the reveal time. It includes the answer only once the week's answer is out.
  - `POST /api/decode/solve {week, answer}` checks the guess and files the solve. The old path (`set solves/<week>_<uid>` with `answer`) goes through the same check.
  - The server writes `city`, `circle`, `cname`, `hints` and `at` itself, and never stores the answer.
  - The page the server hands out (`/`) has the local pack cut out (`DECODE_LOCAL=null`), so it has no salts and no hashes either.
- **Weeks are judged in the person's own time zone.** The zone comes from `people.tz`, held within 3 h of what their longitude says. Hints unlock on the same rule as v0.8, and so does the reveal.
  - Getting the answer early by claiming a far-off zone also closes your own solving early.
  - A sock-puppet account in another zone is the one way round this, and it's documented as such.
- **claude.ai and local mode.** `src/js/34-packs.js` is generated by `node tools/content.js`. For each puzzle it carries:
  - the clue and hints;
  - a per-puzzle salt;
  - `PBKDF2-SHA256(answer, 10k)`, which a guess is checked against on the phone;
  - the answer sealed under a key derived from the salt, opened only after the reveal.

  This mode is an honour system and the sheet says so ("checked on your phone, on your honour"). Grep and view-source show no answer.
- **Ids and city lists never spell an answer.** Puzzle ids are `chennai-01`, not `chennai-marina`, and Howrah is left out of Kolkata's city list. The content test caught both.
- **DEV only:** `GET /api/dev/decode?city=&week=` gives the answer to tests. The legacy e2e ports use it (server mode) or `test/lib/content.js` (local mode, the same schedule as the page) instead of v0.8's `ANS` table.

## Packs (C91)

There are 72 puzzles. Chennai, Bengaluru, Mumbai and Delhi have 12 each; Hyderabad and Kolkata have 6 each; and a generic "Anywhere" pack has 12 for every other city.
- **11 mechanical kinds:** caesar, atbash, a1z26, morse, keypad, reverse, acrostic, last letters, every-other-letter, anagram and hidden word.
- **Every puzzle is solvable by the checker** (`server/content-core.js` `checkPuzzle`). The checker works the answer out of the puzzle's data, checks that the clue shows that data, and checks that no clue, hint or id contains the answer.
- **The table** is in `tools/content/make-puzzles.js`, which writes the JSON.

## No repeats until a pack is used up (C92)

- **Decode, server mode.** Each city gets a puzzle it hasn't had until the pack is used up, picked by an HMAC of a secret.
  - The secret comes from `SEED`, `CONTENT_SECRET`, or one made once and kept in the DB.
  - A new cycle never opens with last week's puzzle.
  - Assignments are stored (`content_sched`), so a week never changes once someone has asked for it.
- **Decode, local mode.** A fixed seeded shuffle per city (`permAt`): every puzzle comes up once in any run of n weeks.
- **Daily dare.** It's the same cycle per circle over the starters (60) or the jar. v0.8 hashed circle + date, which gave 1,320 repeats in 60 30-day runs; v0.9 gives 0.
- **Mole.** You get three tasks you haven't had, kept in your private `molehist`, until you've had all 30. The picks are random, not scheduled, so nobody in the circle can work out the Mole's tasks.

## The drop draw (C50)

- **Commit-reveal.** When a drop is made, the server picks a random seed, keeps it aside (`kv dropseed:<id>`) and publishes only `seedHash = sha256(seed)`. Hosts can't set or replace the seed or the hash, or re-roll by saving again.
- **Reveal.** When the drop closes, the sweep writes `seed` into the drop, and reads after the end include it. The page checks the seed against the hash and says whether it matches.
- **The draw** is a seeded shuffle of the eligible circles: each circle once, only check-ins inside the window. The winner is first place, and later places can carry more prizes, so nobody is drawn twice.
- **A check-in's circle is set by the server** from the person's own record, so nobody can enter another circle.
- **Local mode.** The host's device keeps the seed privately and publishes it after the drop closes.
- **Old drops.** Drops from before v0.9 (no `seedHash`) and trails keep v0.8's formula, so old winners still check out.

## Owner content tool (C93)

`/admin/content` (`server/admin/content.html`) is backed by owner-only `/api/admin/content*` routes. Everyone else gets a 403.
- **Puzzles.** Add or edit a puzzle with Check, which runs the full checker and writes the clue from the data. A puzzle can be pinned to a week and optionally to one city. You can also remove or restore one.
- **Schedule.** Shows what a city gets over the next weeks: a dry run that matches what then happens.
- **Dares and Mole tasks.** Add them through the safety screen. Mole numbers are permanent.
- **Seed drops.** These are published as real drops, hosted by the owner and using the same commit-reveal draw.
- **A third safety floor.** `screenText` refuses traffic, fire, heights, alcohol, police, money, strangers' bodies and similar words in content. WS-D screens what members write.
